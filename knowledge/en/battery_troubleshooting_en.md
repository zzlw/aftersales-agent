## Battery Not Charging

1. **Check the adapter and cable**: make sure you are using the original power adapter and its indicator light is on; try another wall outlet.
2. **Check the charging LED**: with power connected, the charging indicator should be amber (charging) or white (fully charged). If it stays off, go to step 3.
3. **Perform a power drain**: shut down, disconnect the adapter, hold the power button for 15 seconds, then reconnect and boot.
4. **Check battery health**: open Lenovo Vantage → Power → Battery details. If battery health is below 60% or shows "Replacement recommended", request a battery inspection service.
5. **Check Conservation Mode**: when Conservation Mode is enabled in Lenovo Vantage, charging stops at 75%–80% by design — this is not a fault.

If the battery still does not charge after these steps, the battery or the charging circuit on the motherboard may be faulty. Please escalate to a human agent to arrange an inspection.

## Battery Draining Too Fast

- Batteries are consumables; capacity naturally degrades with charge cycles. Retaining about 80% capacity after one year is within the normal range.
- Check high-power background apps in Lenovo Vantage; lowering screen brightness and enabling battery saver extends runtime.
- If a new machine loses more than 50% of its runtime within 3 months, request a battery inspection.

## Swollen Battery Safety Notice

If you notice a swollen battery (deformed chassis, raised touchpad, widening gaps in the bottom cover), **stop using the device and disconnect power immediately**. Do not press or puncture the chassis. Contact official after-sales service as soon as possible — continued use of a swollen battery is a safety hazard.
