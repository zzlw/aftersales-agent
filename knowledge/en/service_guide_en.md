## Official Support Channels

- **Smart assistant** (this system): available 24/7 for troubleshooting, warranty questions, and support ticket creation;
- **Support website**: support.lenovo.com — warranty lookup, driver downloads, online repair requests, and repair status tracking;
- **Phone support**: regional hotline numbers are listed on the support website; business hours vary by country;
- **Authorized service providers**: use the "Service Provider Locator" on the support site to find walk-in locations near you;
- Always use official channels to avoid third-party shops charging inflated fees in the name of "official repair".

## How to Check Warranty Status

1. Locate the machine serial number: label on the bottom cover (notebooks), side panel (desktops), or in Lenovo Vantage;
2. Enter the serial number on the support website to see the warranty start/end date, warranty type, and remaining days;
3. The warranty period is based on the activation date or the invoice date, whichever is later; without proof of purchase, it is estimated from the ship date plus 60 days;
4. If the serial number cannot be found or does not match your model, the unit may be refurbished or tampered — contact official support for verification.

## Tracking a Repair or Support Ticket

- Every submitted request generates a ticket number (starting with "TK"); keep it for reference;
- Enter the ticket number in the smart assistant to check the progress; mail-in repairs include a shipping tracking number;
- Typical turnaround: 1–3 business days for carry-in repair, 5–7 business days for mail-in (including shipping); part back-orders will be notified separately;
- If the promised time is exceeded, call the hotline to expedite or escalate the case.

## Complaints and Escalation

- If you are not satisfied with the service, request an "escalation" in the call or ticket; a specialist will follow up within 48 hours;
- Provide the ticket number, machine serial number, a description of the issue, and your expected resolution;
- For safety incidents (fire, smoke, electric shock), keep photos and the unit itself; such cases are handled with top priority.

## Before Sending Your Device for Repair

- **Back up your data first** — repairs may involve OS reinstallation or storage replacement, and data integrity is not guaranteed;
- Sign out of your accounts and disable device locks (e.g., "Find My Device"); locked devices may be refused for repair;
- Remove SIM cards, memory cards, and personal accessories before handing over the device.
