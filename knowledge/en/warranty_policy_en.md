## Standard Warranty Coverage

- Consumer notebooks come with a **2-year warranty on major components and a 1-year warranty on the whole unit** from the date of purchase. ThinkPad commercial series includes 3-year on-site service (refer to your warranty card).
- Major components include: motherboard, CPU, memory, HDD/SSD, graphics card, and power adapter.
- **The battery is a consumable part with a 1-year warranty.** During the warranty period, a battery with health below 60% (without man-made damage) is eligible for a free replacement.

## International Warranty Service (IWS)

- Selected ThinkPad and consumer models support International Warranty Service. You can obtain warranty service in any country where the product is sold and IWS is available.
- Service scope may vary by country; parts availability and response time follow the local service standard.
- To check IWS eligibility, provide your machine serial number to the support agent or query it on the official support site.

## Screen Dead Pixel Standard

- During the warranty period, a screen with **3 or more bright/dark pixels**, or 1 bright pixel located in the central area, qualifies for a free screen replacement.
- Fewer than 3 dead pixels outside the central area falls within the industry-accepted range and is not covered.

## What Is NOT Covered

1. Man-made damage: liquid spill, drops, crushing, or unauthorized disassembly/modification;
2. Failures after the warranty period expires (paid repair available);
3. Products purchased from unauthorized channels without a valid proof of purchase;
4. Damage caused by force majeure such as natural disasters.

## How to Get Warranty Service

1. Prepare your proof of purchase (invoice or order screenshot) and the machine serial number (label on the bottom cover, or in Lenovo Vantage);
2. Submit a request via the official hotline, the online support portal, or this smart assistant (a support ticket will be created);
3. Consumer models support carry-in or mail-in repair; commercial models support on-site service;
4. Typical repair turnaround is 5–7 business days. Round-trip shipping for mail-in repair is covered under warranty.

## Return and Exchange Policy

- Within **7 days** of delivery: with a verified performance defect, you may choose refund, replacement, or repair;
- Within **15 days** of delivery: with a verified performance defect, you may choose replacement or repair;
- No-reason returns within 7 days require the product to be intact with full packaging and accessories. OS activation may affect eligibility (subject to the purchase platform's policy).
